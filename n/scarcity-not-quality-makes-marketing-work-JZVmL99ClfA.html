Scarcity, not quality, makes marketing work

2026-09-20 / Watch

Use

  1. Test your marketing plan against the 10x cost rule — Would you still do this if it cost ten times more? If no, it's easy and everyone's doing it; cut it or reshape it. If yes, friction is already baked in.
  2. Audit your unfair advantages — Identify what nobody can copy or commodify (credentials, connections, proven track record, unique access, genuine skill) and center every marketing move around those, not what you're best at writing about.
  3. Shift from additive to displacement strategy — Stop asking "would this be valuable?" and start asking "what does this replace?" You're not asking for new mental space in a saturated attention market; you're displacing something the audience already does.

Bottom line

Marketing stopped working because supply exploded while attention stayed fixed, collapsing trust and forcing audiences into skepticism. The only fix is the opposite of every recent industry advice: make marketing difficult, expensive, and leveraged on advantages nobody can copy — because scarcity, not quality, is what now works.

Key ideas

  • Scarcity determines value; abundance determines worthlessness — once something is abundant, quality stops mattering entirely, even if the thing is objectively good. No amount of quality breaks into a consciousness already full.11:57
  • Human bandwidth is the fixed constraint — only so many hours, dollars, and attention units exist; the app store graph shows production booming while downloads flatline, proving overwhelming choice makes people switch off and retreat to trusted sources.3:07
  • Every marketing channel follows a lifecycle: scarcity → pioneer wins → copying → saturation → death — pioneers won not because they were best but because they were almost alone; AI didn't start this, it just accelerated the final collapse.5:13
  • Technology's 20-year war on friction buried, not freed us — removing difficulty to make, ship, and scale everything lowered the barrier to entry and flooded every channel with noise; the old friction forced focus and interrogation; removing it killed that forcing function.8:17
  • Make it unfair — deploy advantages nobody can copy or hand out: genuine expertise, specific credentials, proven track record, celebrity attachment, unique access, real skill on camera, or deep connections. Center marketing around what only you can do.16:05
  • Make it tedious and expensive — handwritten letters, commissioned research, speaking tours, hiring agencies, published books, produced video at scale, live events. The friction is the message; most won't pay the cost, which is exactly why paying it works.19:10
  • Replace something, don't ask to be added — treat a saturated market as displacement, not addition; think about what you're displacing, not what you're adding; create new needs in existing spaces while replacing old ones, like Liquid Death or Trader Joe's.24:18
